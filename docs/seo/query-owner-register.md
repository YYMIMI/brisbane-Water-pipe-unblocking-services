## Cross-site boundary

- \`melonedrains.com.au\` is the only primary owner for Brisbane drain cleaning, blocked-drain and sewer-cleaning queries.
- \`meloneplumbingvic.com.au\` owns general Brisbane plumbing, pipe leaks, taps, toilets, hot water and urgent plumbing enquiries.
- A toilet mechanism, tap or supply-pipe fault belongs to Plumbing; a toilet or fixture that will not drain belongs to Drains.
- Old drain URLs on the Plumbing domain permanently redirect to the matching Drains owner page.
- Other plumbing repairs are linked to the Plumbing service hub so customers are not trapped on the wrong site.

## Response and conversion

- Customer enquiries are answered within 24 hours.
- Same-day or urgent site attendance is confirmed separately and is not implied by the 24-hour reply promise.
- GA4 click events record link type and page path only; phone numbers and email addresses are not sent as event parameters.
